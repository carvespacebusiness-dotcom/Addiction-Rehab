**Rehabilitation Support – Creating a Structured Recovery Environment**

Appropriate rehabilitation support can help individuals work towards improved emotional wellbeing, healthier routines and greater independence.
A comprehensive rehabilitation programme should address individual requirements rather than follow the same approach for everyone.
What Can Rehabilitation Support Include?
✦ Initial physical and psychological assessment.
✦ Personalised rehabilitation planning.
✦ Counselling and therapeutic interventions.
✦ Structured daily activities.
✦ Behavioural and emotional support.
✦ Medical monitoring when necessary.
✦ Family education and participation.
✦ Discharge and follow-up planning.
Importance of Individualised Care
The duration and intensity of rehabilitation may vary according to the individual's condition and treatment response.
Professional monitoring can help identify progress and determine whether additional support is required.
